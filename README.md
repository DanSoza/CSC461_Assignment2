## VR Car Museum

This projects main purpose is to educate those who experience (users), about the history of significant performance cars. This museum allows users to navigate the room and learn a little about each vehicle.

## Exhibitions

The museum contains five car exhibitions:

- **Ford GT (2005)** – The history of the Ford GT and its connection to the Ford GT40 and the 24 Hours of Le Mans.
- **Nissan Silvia S15 (1999–2002)** – The history of the Silvia and its significance in Japanese car and drifting culture.
- **Toyota Supra A80 (1993–2002)** – The fourth-generation Supra, including the 2JZ-GTE engine and its influence on tuning culture.
- **Nissan Skyline GT-R R34 (1999–2002)** – The history and engineering of the R34 GT-R, including its RB26DETT engine and all-wheel-drive system.
- **Honda NSX (1990–2005)** – Honda's development of a lightweight, reliable supercar and the NSX's use of aluminum construction and VTEC technology.

## VR Interaction

The museum uses VR controllers for navigation and interaction.

Each exhibition includes an information panel that provides educational information about the vehicle. When the user approaches an exhibition, a proximity-based event is triggered. The vehicle's information panel appears and an engine sound plays (the only accurate engine sound is the gt40 due to limited assets for engine sounds).
